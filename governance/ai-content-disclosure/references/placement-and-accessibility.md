# Placement and accessibility

- Make the icon perceivable and distinguishable no later than first exposure.
- Avoid intervening overlays.
- Embed the icon into downloadable or reshareable visual content when appropriate.
- Use a clearly visible size and sufficient contrast.
- Do not cover important content, faces, captions, or existing notices.
- Add alt text or ARIA labels when the channel supports them.
- Use plain-language accompanying text.
- Do not stretch, crop, redraw, or recolour the official icon.

The scripts use bottom-right placement by default, a 3% image-width icon size, and a 2% safe margin. These are operational defaults, not universal legal safe harbours.
