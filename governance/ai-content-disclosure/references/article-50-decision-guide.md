# Article 50 decision guide

## Scope reminder

Article 50 transparency obligations apply differently to providers and deployers.

For deployers, visible disclosure under Article 50(4) principally concerns:

- deep fake image, audio, or video content;
- AI-generated or manipulated text published to inform the public on matters of public interest, when it has not undergone human review or editorial control and no natural or legal person assumes editorial responsibility.

Not all AI-generated or AI-assisted content requires visible labelling under Article 50.

## Decision sequence

1. Is AI-generated or AI-manipulated content involved?
2. Is the actor a provider, deployer, publisher, or another role?
3. Is the content a deep fake as legally defined?
4. For text, is it published to inform the public on a matter of public interest?
5. For such text, was there human review or editorial control and assumed editorial responsibility?
6. Does an artistic, creative, satirical, fictional, criminal-law, or other exception apply?
7. Is a visible disclosure required, potentially required, or merely recommended?
8. Are separate machine-readable provider obligations relevant?

Escalate ambiguous legal classification rather than presenting false certainty.
