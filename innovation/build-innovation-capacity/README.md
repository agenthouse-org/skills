An executive skill for diagnosing where organizational attention is consumed and creating practical capacity for innovation.

The skill operationalizes the **Alpha Pyramid**, developed by Valerio Neri in the book *Die Alpha Pyramide*. It explains the framework in plain English so users do not need prior knowledge of the book.

## What it helps with

- distinguish operational work, implementation, and exploratory innovation;
- diagnose operational overload and innovation stagnation;
- expose assumptions behind strategy and transformation programs;
- release capacity without undermining essential operations;
- move unconventional ideas into bounded experiments and implementation;
- design executive conversations and leadership workshops.

## Core model

- **RUN** keeps the organization operating.
- **BUILD** creates and implements something new.
- **ALPHA** challenges accepted assumptions and explores unconventional possibilities.

## Example prompt

```text
Use the Build Innovation Capacity skill to diagnose why our organization discusses innovation but rarely implements it. Explain the model in plain language, classify our work, identify the binding constraint, and propose three practical interventions with validation steps.
```
