# Worked Example: Established Company Without Innovation Time

## Situation

An established company plans every specialist at full utilization. Improvement ideas are welcomed, but managers repeatedly defer them to a future quarter because customer delivery consumes all available capacity.

## Diagnosis

The organization treats maximum planned utilization as efficiency. This makes both BUILD and ALPHA dependent on overtime. The main risk is prescribing a fixed percentage without understanding economics and delivery obligations.

## Intervention

1. Identify recurring RUN that can be stopped, simplified, or automated.
2. Create a four-week pilot with protected capacity in one team.
3. Select one operational improvement and one customer-facing hypothesis.
4. Track delivery performance, released effort, learning, and interruptions.

## Result interpretation

- If delivery remains stable and learning improves, expand cautiously.
- If urgent RUN repeatedly consumes the reserve, diagnose the sources of instability.
- If the team has capacity but no testable ideas, strengthen ALPHA prompts and customer discovery.

## Executive answer

Do not begin by mandating a company-wide innovation percentage. Pilot protected capacity in one team, pair it with explicit work removal, and evaluate both operational safety and the quality of learning.
