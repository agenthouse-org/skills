# Worked Example: Corporate DNA Growth Workshop

## Situation

A mature B2B manufacturer wants adjacent growth opportunities but assumes it lacks the digital capabilities required to create them.

## Workshop

1. Capture customer segments and buying-center relationships.
2. Capture active, unused, and learnable employee skills.
3. Capture reputation: why customers trust the company.
4. Capture core processes and digital and physical assets.
5. Recombine cards in a cross-functional group.
6. Record promising concepts and mark missing capabilities as gaps.

## Example combination

- Trusted compliance reputation
- Installed equipment base
- Field-service relationships
- Unused data-analysis skills

Possible concept: a compliance-monitoring service for installed equipment.

## Validation

Do not treat the combination as a business case. Interview relevant customers, verify the problem and willingness to engage, inspect data rights and technical feasibility, and test a manual service before building a platform.

## Executive answer

The workshop's value is not the number of ideas. It makes existing capabilities visible, creates novel combinations, and distinguishes gaps that must be bought, built, learned, or accepted before investment.
