# Leadership Workshop: Create Capacity for Innovation

## Intended outcome

Agree on the current RUN-BUILD-ALPHA pattern, identify the binding constraint, and authorize one to three bounded interventions.

## Recommended participants

Executive sponsor, relevant functional leaders, people close to representative work, and an independent facilitator when useful.

## Preparation

- Collect a representative list of recurring work and initiatives.
- Sample leadership calendars and recurring escalations.
- Select one idea or initiative to trace end to end.
- Note critical operational, legal, safety, and financial constraints.

## Agenda

1. **Decision and evidence — 15 min**  
   Define the decision, symptoms, and known facts.

2. **Plain-language framework — 15 min**  
   Explain RUN, BUILD, and ALPHA with company-specific examples.

3. **Classification — 35 min**  
   Classify representative work and mark mixed or disputed items.

4. **Flow diagnosis — 30 min**  
   Trace an idea from ALPHA through BUILD into RUN.

5. **Provocation — 25 min**  
   Use two selected ALPHA Questions.

6. **Interventions — 30 min**  
   Choose the smallest actions addressing the binding constraint.

7. **Commitment — 20 min**  
   Assign owners, tests, safeguards, and review dates.

## Outputs

- classified work sample;
- binding-constraint statement;
- selected capacity interventions;
- experiment cards;
- decision and review date.
