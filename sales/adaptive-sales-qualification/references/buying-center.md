# Buying-Center Assessment

## Purpose

Complex purchases are made by a system of participants, not by a single title.

## Roles to Identify

| Role | Core question |
|---|---|
| Beneficiary | Who receives the business value? |
| User | Who will use or operate the solution? |
| Champion | Who actively helps the seller progress? |
| Problem owner | Who is accountable for the current problem? |
| Decision maker | Who selects or authorizes the solution? |
| Economic approver | Who controls or approves funding? |
| Technical approver | Who validates technical feasibility? |
| Security or compliance approver | Who can block on risk grounds? |
| Procurement | Who manages commercial and procedural compliance? |
| Influencer | Who shapes requirements or preferences? |
| Blocker | Who could prevent or delay the decision? |
| Signatory | Who can legally execute the agreement? |

One person may hold several roles. One role may also be shared by several people.

## Evidence Questions

- Is the role verified or inferred?
- Does the person have formal authority, informal influence, or both?
- What outcome does this role value?
- What risks does this role fear?
- Is the role engaged directly, indirectly, or not at all?
- Who introduced the seller to this role?
- What action has the role taken?
- Is there disagreement between participants?

## Champion Test

Do not label a contact as a champion merely because the person is friendly.

A credible champion usually:

- has influence or access;
- benefits from change;
- shares internal context appropriately;
- helps navigate the process;
- takes reciprocal action;
- is willing to advocate when the seller is absent.

## Buying-Center Risks

Highlight:

- single-threaded access;
- contact without influence;
- unidentified economic approval;
- hidden incumbent support;
- user enthusiasm without management support;
- executive interest without operational ownership;
- procurement engagement before value is established;
- late security, legal, or works-council involvement;
- conflicting success criteria.
