# Qualification Model

## Purpose

This model assesses four related but distinct objects:

1. lead;
2. account;
3. opportunity;
4. combined pursuit case.

Each dimension is scored from 0 to 5:

- **0 — disproven or unacceptable**
- **1 — very weak**
- **2 — weak or mostly unknown**
- **3 — plausible**
- **4 — strong**
- **5 — verified and compelling**

A score without evidence is incomplete. Every score must include an evidence label.

## Evidence Labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified | Supported by a reliable source or direct observation |
| Buyer-reported | Stated by the prospect |
| Seller-reported | Stated internally |
| Inferred | Reasonable inference, not confirmed |
| Unknown | No reliable information |
| Contradictory | Material sources disagree |

## Lead Assessment

Assess the individual contact, not the entire company.

| Dimension | Core question |
|---|---|
| Relevance | Is this person connected to the problem or buying process? |
| Role | What buying-center role could this person hold? |
| Access | Can the seller communicate and progress through this person? |
| Engagement quality | Is engagement specific, reciprocal, and meaningful? |
| Credibility | Is contact and role information reliable? |
| Timing signal | Is there a credible reason for current engagement? |

A friendly or senior contact is not automatically a qualified lead.

## Account Assessment

| Dimension | Core question |
|---|---|
| ICP fit | Does the account match the target profile? |
| Problem plausibility | Is the relevant problem likely to exist at meaningful scale? |
| Economic fit | Can the account support the commercial model? |
| Operational fit | Can the product be implemented and used? |
| Geographic fit | Can the seller contract, deliver, and support locally? |
| Regulatory fit | Are legal and compliance constraints manageable? |
| Strategic fit | Does the account create strategic value? |
| Risk profile | Are credit, reputation, delivery, or concentration risks acceptable? |

## Opportunity Assessment

| Dimension | Core question |
|---|---|
| Problem evidence | Is a material problem verified? |
| Value case | Is the likely value sufficient and credible? |
| Solution fit | Does the offering address the actual need? |
| Urgency | Why act now rather than later? |
| Buying center | Are key roles identified and engaged? |
| Decision process | Is the decision path understood? |
| Funding and economics | Is spending capacity or an approved funding path credible? |
| Competition | Is the competitive position understood when relevant? |
| Delivery fit | Can the seller credibly deliver? |
| Mutual commitment | Is the buyer investing time, access, data, or action? |
| Next-step quality | Is there a concrete, reciprocal next step? |
| Risk and contradiction | Are blockers and conflicting signals understood? |

## Combined Assessment

Use company weights when configured.

Default stage-sensitive weights:

| Situation | Lead | Account | Opportunity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Early lead | 35% | 45% | 20% |
| Active opportunity | 20% | 30% | 50% |
| Strategic opportunity | 10% | 25% | 65% |

Calculate a weighted score only after applying hard rules.

### Suggested interpretation

| Score | Interpretation |
|---:|---|
| 80–100 | Strong qualification |
| 65–79 | Qualified with material verification needs |
| 45–64 | Conditional, nurture, or limited investment |
| 25–44 | Weak pursuit case |
| 0–24 | Disqualify or no-bid |

These bands are defaults, not universal truths.

## Gating Factors

Do not let averages hide critical weaknesses. Treat these as possible gates:

- prohibited use case;
- unsupported delivery;
- unacceptable legal or compliance risk;
- no verified problem after appropriate discovery;
- no plausible buying path;
- uneconomic cost of sale;
- mandatory tender requirement cannot be met;
- material conflict of interest;
- unacceptable payment or credit risk.

## Qualification Status

### Qualified

Use when the case meets company requirements and no critical gate is open.

### Conditionally qualified

Use when the case is attractive but one or more material issues require verification.

### Nurture

Use when fit exists but timing, evidence, access, or economics do not justify active pursuit.

### Disqualified

Use when a hard rule applies or the pursuit case is materially invalid.

### No-bid

Use for a tender or RFP that should not receive bid investment.

### Insufficient evidence

Use when a responsible decision cannot yet be made and the missing evidence is material.

## Priority

Priority considers more than qualification:

- expected value;
- probability;
- cost of sale;
- strategic value;
- timing;
- delivery capacity;
- opportunity cost.

A qualified opportunity may still be low priority.
