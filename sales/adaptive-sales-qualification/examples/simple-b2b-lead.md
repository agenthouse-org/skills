# Example: Simple B2B Lead

## Scenario

A 40-person professional-services company downloads a guide about automating proposal creation. A managing consultant requests a demo. The product costs approximately €300 per month, onboarding is simple, and the normal sales cycle is two to four weeks.

- **Last meaningful communication:** Demo request submitted this week

- **Top positive signal:** Relevant contact requested a concrete next step
- **Top risk or contradiction:** No verified pain, proposal volume, urgency, or approval path yet
- **Commercial viability summary:** Likely economical to continue with a short, low-cost discovery step
- **Recommendation summary:** Continue, but limit effort to lightweight discovery until need and buying path are verified
- **Recommended next action:** Run a short discovery call focused on current workflow, pain, volume, and approval path

## Expanded Assessment

### Decision Summary

- **Lead:** Strong relevance and credible engagement
- **Account:** Plausible fit, but process volume is unknown
- **Opportunity:** Early hypothesis, not yet verified

### Rationale

The contact has a relevant role and took a meaningful action. The account appears operationally suitable. However, no current pain, proposal volume, decision process, or urgency has been verified.

### Sales Activity Footprint

- **Source:** Inbound lead
- **Sales motion so far:** Feature-led entry point, with consultative discovery still required
- **Activity level:** Low but appropriate for this stage
- **Last communication:** Recent demo request
- **Discovery progression:** Minimal verified learning so far
- **Interpretation:** The lead is worth engaging, but current activity is still only a starting signal, not proof of a real opportunity

### Recommendations

- **Pursuit recommendation:** Continue with lightweight pursuit
- **Discovery recommendation:** Verify current proposal process, pain intensity, and expected value
- **Stakeholder recommendation:** Confirm whether the managing consultant is also a user, champion, or approver
- **Commercial recommendation:** Keep effort low until monthly proposal volume and willingness to pay are clearer
- **Risk-management recommendation:** Do not forecast aggressively until urgency and decision process are verified

### Next Action

Run a short discovery focused on current proposal volume, time cost, existing tooling, and who approves a subscription.

### High-Value Questions

1. How many proposals are created each month?
2. What currently makes proposal creation costly or slow?
3. Who would approve and use the system?
