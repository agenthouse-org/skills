# Example: High-Consideration B2C

## Scenario

A household is considering a high-value home energy system involving financing, installation, local permitting, and a long-term service agreement.

## Assessment Summary

- **Mode:** Standard
- **Lead:** Genuine interest and property ownership verified
- **Account equivalent:** Household and property appear technically suitable
- **Opportunity:** Need and suitability are plausible; affordability and co-decision-maker alignment are unknown
- **Status:** Conditionally qualified
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Probability:** 20–40%
- **Confidence:** Low to medium

## Guardrails

Do not infer income, health, ethnicity, age-related vulnerability, or other sensitive traits. Verify affordability and suitability through appropriate, consent-based questions and formal processes.

## Next Action

Complete a technical suitability assessment and confirm the decision participants, financing preference, total-cost understanding, and installation timeline.
