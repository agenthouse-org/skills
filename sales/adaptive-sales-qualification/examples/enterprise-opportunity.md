# Example: Enterprise Opportunity

## Scenario

A multinational manufacturer is considering an AI-supported knowledge system. The initial contact is the head of process excellence. IT security, data protection, works council, procurement, and several business units are likely to be involved. Estimated contract value is €180,000 annually plus implementation.

## Assessment Summary

- **Mode:** Strategic
- **Lead:** Relevant and influential, but champion strength unverified
- **Account:** Strong fit with material delivery and governance complexity
- **Opportunity:** Credible problem, incomplete buying process
- **Status:** Conditionally qualified
- **Priority:** High, with controlled investment
- **Probability:** 20–35%
- **Confidence:** Medium

## Main Risks

- economic approver not identified;
- success criteria are qualitative;
- security and works-council process not mapped;
- implementation ownership is unclear;
- no reciprocal evaluation plan exists.

## Next Action

Agree a mutual validation plan covering business case, pilot scope, security review, works-council requirements, stakeholders, and decision dates.

## Exit Condition

Reduce priority if the buyer will not provide stakeholder access or define a decision and validation process.
