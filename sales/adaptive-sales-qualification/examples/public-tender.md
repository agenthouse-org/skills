# Example: Public Tender

## Scenario

A software company discovers a public tender ten days before the deadline. It meets most functional requirements but lacks one requested reference. The specification closely resembles an incumbent platform. There was no prior discovery or formal market consultation. Preparing the bid would require approximately eight person-days.

## Assessment Summary

- **Mode:** Strategic
- **Seller position:** Reactive participant
- **Mandatory fit:** Uncertain because of the missing reference
- **Competitive position:** Disadvantaged
- **Status:** No-bid unless the reference requirement can lawfully be satisfied
- **Priority:** Do not pursue
- **Probability:** 3–10%
- **Confidence:** Medium

## Rationale

The seller has limited customer context, late entry, a possible mandatory gap, a specification advantage for the incumbent, and disproportionate bid effort. Meeting functional requirements alone does not create a credible pursuit case.

## Compliance Boundary

The company may use the formal clarification channel to determine whether equivalent evidence is accepted. It must not seek privileged information or attempt to influence the published requirements outside the permitted process.

## Reconsideration Trigger

Reassess only if:

- the reference requirement is non-mandatory or equivalent evidence is accepted;
- a material differentiator maps to weighted criteria;
- bid effort can be reduced;
- or new lawful information changes the competitive position.
